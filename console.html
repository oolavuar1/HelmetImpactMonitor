1 · Gravity check

Checks that the accelerometer's scale and axes are right, using gravity as a known 1 g (9.81 m/s²). Stop the system, set the device flat and still, press Read. Repeat with each of the 6 faces pointing down. The total should read about 9.81 every time.

3 · Accuracy drops (impulse check)

Checks whether an impact that should read 80 g actually reads 80 g. A drop from height h hits at a known speed, √(2gh). Adding up the acceleration over the whole impact has to give that speed, plus however fast it bounced back, so the total should land between 1× and 2× the impact speed. Well under 1× means the sensor is reading low.
Stop the system, enter the height, press Arm, then drop. Keep the device side off the ground.

5 · Rotation check (angle)

Checks that the gyroscope reads rotation speed correctly. If it does, adding up its readings over a turn (speed × time) has to equal the angle you actually turned, however fast or slow you went. Stop the system, line the helmet up with your start mark, enter the angle, press Arm and keep it still for half a second while it zeroes. Turn it in one smooth motion and stop lined up on the end mark. It stops on its own once the helmet has been slower than the turning speed for half a second. Any nudge after that isn't counted, so stop cleanly on the mark. Stop (manual) is there as a backup.
Every turn is measured two ways. Whole turn: rotation is added up from Arm until it stops. Threshold: only samples faster than the "turning" speed count; turning time = those samples ÷ the sample rate, and the gyro's average speed over that time is compared to the true angle ÷ that time.
